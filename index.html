03 / THE STORY

About

Defacto are a Bristol-based indie rock band originally formed in 1996. Through the release of numerous EPs and albums over the years, they have established a distinctive presence on the live music scene.

Defacto brings together Marc, Ellie, Cerith, and Ollie. Their latest release, Blue Eyes and Violins, has been 30 years in the making and explores how love can alleviate destructive behaviors and relieve the feeling of isolation.

Following an extended hiatus, the band return in 2026 marking the start of a new chapter in their story. The band is currently working on new material whilst promoting the release of Blue Eyes and Violins. For bookings, interviews and press enquiries, use the contact details below.

“Music that comes from halfway between Goth, Techno, A Clockwork Orange and some spooky cinematic wodges of sound, heavily fulled by rampant guitars and emphatic vocals.” — Hex Files: The Goth Bible / Mick Mercer
